# Shadow of the Old Ones: art list

Style for every piece: same as the Lower Archive (chunky pixel art, limited palette, dark outlines,
three-quarter top-down for maps). Old Ones stay natural animals (trilobites, octopuses, sea scorpions),
just ancient and huge. Smilodons are natural saber-toothed cats, never humanoid.

## Batch 1: the way down (DONE 2026-11-10: maps/library_lower_open.png, maps/old_stairwell.png, maps/old_city_reveal.png)
1. **Vault open**: the Lower Archive vault door rolled aside, huge old stairs going down, purple light,
   saber-tooth skull on the keystone. Pasted into a full copy of the map, `maps/library_lower_open.png`,
   which the hub switches to with `openIf:'oldOnesOpen'` + `imgOpen`.
2. **Descent stairwell** (landscape 1536x1024, full screen; the portrait version was rejected): a long zigzag stair carved for giant paws,
   relief carvings of smilodons hunting giant trilobites on the walls, purple crystal veins, getting
   darker towards the bottom. The camera pans down it while Puiutu walks down.
3. **City reveal** (wide): the last step opens onto a ledge over a huge cavern with the Smilodon city
   (stepped stone halls, giant fang statues, dead crystal forges, a dark sea at the far end).
