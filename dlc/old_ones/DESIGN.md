# Shadow of the Old Ones: zone 1, the City of Fangs (proposal)

Target: ~35-45 min of the DLC's ~2h. One hub map (dlc/old_ones/art/city_of_fangs.png).

## Layout (left to right)
- Foot of the Old Stairs (arrival) + an abandoned camp
- Mural wall (lore), first sphinx statue, skull mosaic
- The avenue into the dark (closed: "the rest of the city", later content)
- Second sphinx statue, the sealed Forge Gate (3 round sockets)
- The pigs' survey camp
- Broken stair down to the black sea (exit to zone 3, blocked by rubble for now)

## The Forge Gate: three Fang Cores
- **Purple: the Weaver** (boss, Avenue of Fangs). A giant tarantula with one purple crystal eye. It lives in the
  hole in the roof and drops on its thread when you walk down the avenue; walks and bites, rears and lunges,
  and below half health climbs back up and slams down where you stand (its shadow warns you). Beaten, it
  curls up asleep (not dead) and the purple eye comes loose. `S.oldTarantulaDown`.
- **Orange: Pip** (plaza): three crystal samples for his report. Snout's Survey Badge skips the paperwork.
- **Green: Whisk** (the alcove on the avenue, only talks once the Weaver is down): bring his pack from the camp.
- The cores light the gate one by one: purple, orange, green.

## The Avenue of Fangs (zone 1b)
Built to match the title screen: stepped buildings, ivory fang pillars, the forge, two rows of braziers with
the mosaic avenue between them, webs and the hole in the roof. A fallen sphinx head blocks the far end.

## NPCs
- **The rat deserter** (companion for zone 1), at the camp by the stairs
- **Survey Clerk + Agent Snout** (pigs), at the tent
- **The Union soldier** (cat), arrives once you have the first core
- **Old Fang** (optional): the spirit of the last smilodon, a pale see-through natural big cat. It
  appears by the murals and tells the story of the crystals in short lines.

## Enemies
- **Trilobite crawlers:** small, armoured, come out of the cracks. The common enemy.
- **Shard moths:** small flying swarms that live on the crystal veins.
- **Fang Sentinel:** a stone smilodon statue still on guard (elite). On the true route you pull out
  its crystal instead of breaking it.
- Teaser: a sea scorpion glimpsed at the black sea stair (zone 3).

## Lore found here (murals, 4)
1. The smilodons find the crystals. 2. The crystals are cut out of the Old Ones.
3. The weapons. 4. The fall of the city.
