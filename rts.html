How to play

Workers gather fish (from the ponds) and wood (from the trees) and bring it to your town hall. Spend it on buildings, units, upgrades and your hero. Houses give supply. Neutral camps guard the expansions: kill them for fish, hero experience and items. The Pig Exchange in the middle hires mercenaries to anyone who brings a unit close. Destroy every enemy building to win.
