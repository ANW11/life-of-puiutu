This is Puiutu.

A small tabby cat.
No army. No flag. No opinion on taxes.

He likes fish, naps in the sun and his best friend Rocky. Then the war reaches his town square, and suddenly everyone wants something from a cat who just wanted lunch.

Puiutu is a real cat.
He has no idea he is in a video game.

Not made up.

They are all real.

Every main character in the game is a real animal from the developer's life. Same stripes, same faces, same opinions about dinner.

Pick a side.

The Feline Union

"Freedom. Unity. Summits."

Its king is a lion. Everybody says so. Nobody you know has ever actually seen him up close.

"Fight for us, little one."

The Rat Federation

"This land was always ours."

Rattus Magnus. His portrait hangs on every wall in the Warren. Every portrait looks a little different.

"Fight for us, little one."

Same words. Same mouths. Or don't.

A cozy little life.
Mostly.

Fish it.

From clownfish to great whites. Some of them are bigger than you. Reel anyway.

Fight it.

Crayfish queens, guardian spirits, things in the deep with too many eyes.

Get rich. Or don't.

Trade, dig, sort, solve. The bank is always happy to see you. Suspiciously happy.

Make friends.

A loyal Westie, a pig with a badge, a rat who stopped marching. They walk with you and they talk back.

Play together.

Meet other players in the town square, fish side by side, or wage a tiny cat and rat war in Border War.

Three endings.

Serve the cats. Serve the rats. Or find out who serves whom.

Be any cat you want.

A whole world to wander.

Confidential

Somebody is paying for both sides.

Same weapons. Same crates. Same little seal stamped on every one of them. The war is not a war. It is a business.

Animals who fight each other never look up.
