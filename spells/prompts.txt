 PROMPTURI ASSETS — ICOANE DE SPELURI (Life of Puiutu)
 Stil: cozy 32-bit pixel art, Stardew-meets-children's-book
 Sfat: atașează-i lui Gemini o captură din joc (bara de speluri sau
 un reveal de gacha) ca referință de stil înainte de a cere icoanele.

CUM FUNCȚIONEAZĂ ÎN JOC
- Codul caută automat  spells/<id>.png  (numele exacte mai jos).
- Dacă fișierul lipsește, se afișează emoji-ul ca rezervă — deci poți
  genera icoanele pe rând și testa după fiecare, fără să aștepți tot setul.
- Icoana apare în 3 locuri: bara de speluri (30px), spellbook din
  inventar (44px) și magazin (44px), plus mare de tot în reveal-ul de
  gacha pentru Fish Storm și Nine Lives.
- IMPORTANT: pentru că se vede și la 30px, cere-i explicit forme
  SIMPLE și citibile, cu contrast mare — nu detalii mărunte.

REGULI DE PUS ÎN FIECARE PROMPT (copy-paste la final):
- "solid flat lime-green background (pure RGB 0,255,0), completely
  uniform, no gradient, no shadow on the background"
  (tu scoți verdele; jocul primește PNG deja transparent)
- "no text, no labels, no watermark, no border, no frame, no grid"
- "single centered object filling about 80% of the square canvas"
- "chunky readable pixel-art shapes with bold dark outlines, high
  contrast, must stay legible when scaled down to 30x30 pixels"
- "cozy warm storybook palette, cute not scary — this is a game about
  a kitten"
- 512x512, square

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HAIRBALL VOLLEY  →  spells/hairball.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art spell icon, 512x512. A swirling ball of tangled brown-and-
ginger cat fur flying forward, with two small motion swooshes behind it
and a few loose fur strands trailing. Cute and slightly silly, not
gross. Warm brown/amber palette.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ZOOMIES  →  spells/zoomies.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art spell icon, 512x512. A bright yellow lightning-bolt dash
streak shaped like a fast motion trail, with a tiny cat-paw silhouette
at its leading tip and small speed lines behind. Conveys sudden
blink-forward speed. Electric yellow and warm white palette.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CATNIP CLOUD  →  spells/catnip.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art spell icon, 512x512. A puffy green cloud of catnip haze with
a few small catnip leaves floating inside it and tiny sparkle dots.
Soft rounded cloud shape, dreamy and playful. Fresh green palette.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PURR WARD  →  spells/purrward.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art spell icon, 512x512. A glowing pale-blue protective bubble
shield, translucent, with a soft highlight arc on the upper left and
three tiny sound-wave arcs (a purr) radiating inside it. Calm, cozy,
protective. Ice-blue and white palette.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CRYSTAL SPIKES  →  spells/crystalspikes.png   (drop de la Queen Seby)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art spell icon, 512x512. A cluster of sharp violet crystal shards
erupting upward from a small cracked ground base, the tallest shard in
the middle, faint magical glow and a couple of floating sparkle motes.
Violet and cyan palette, mystical but still cute.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THUNDER PAW  →  spells/thunderpaw.png   (drop de la King)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art spell icon, 512x512. A cat paw print made of crackling
electric-blue lightning, with a jagged bolt arcing out of it to one
side. Regal and powerful but friendly. Electric blue and pale gold
palette.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
7. FISH STORM  →  spells/fishstorm.png   (gacha EPIC — se vede și mare)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art spell icon, 512x512. Three or four cartoon fish raining down
diagonally out of a small blue storm cloud, with tiny water droplets
between them. Absurd and funny on purpose. Blue and silver palette with
warm orange fish. Extra polish welcome — this one is also shown large
in a gacha reveal card.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
8. NINE LIVES  →  spells/ninelives.png   (gacha LEGENDARY — se vede și mare)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art spell icon, 512x512. A radiant golden cat silhouette sitting
inside a glowing halo/aura, with the numeral 9 subtly formed by the
swirl of the aura around it, plus small golden sparkles. Warm, magical,
triumphant — the rarest prize in the game. Gold, cream and soft amber
palette. Make this the most impressive of the eight.
